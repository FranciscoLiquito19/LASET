\section*{Abstract}
This report describes the design of a multi-protocol read/write unit that
connects a PC to external devices over I2C, RS232, RS485, two CAN FD buses
and Ethernet, and drives three isolated PWM outputs, with time stamping
synchronised to a GPS PPS signal. The unit routes data between interfaces
with different rates, keeps making progress under concurrent load and
stores its configuration in non-volatile memory.

Starting from the needs gathered with the supervisors, the report defines
seven use cases, 18 functional and 8 non-functional requirements with
their acceptance criteria, and a high-level architecture. In the design
phase, an STM32H743 microcontroller running FreeRTOS is selected, together
with isolated transceivers for CAN FD, RS485 and PWM and a buck-boost
supply that accepts 5 to 60 V. The complete schematic is generated from
code and checked automatically, and the firmware is organised as tasks
with one queue per interface and two priority classes.

\section*{Keywords}
Multi-protocol gateway, CAN FD, RS485, Ethernet, GPS PPS, isolated PWM,
STM32H743, FreeRTOS.
